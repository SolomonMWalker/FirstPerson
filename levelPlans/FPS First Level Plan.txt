FPS First Level Plan

WHAT WE'RE MAKING
    The first level of the Bert Vs The Devil Game
    Fight through a small town taken over by the Demon Fascists
        get to the retirement home where Auntie Aggy lives
        get her wheelchair out of her van
        get Aggy back home
    Showcase the enemies, two weapons, and mechanics

ELEMENTS
    Setting - small town strip next to a train track, taken over by demon fascists
        retirement home relatively unscathed, but the demon fascists are there and shits getting rough
    Enemies
        grunt with pistol
        demon dog
        exploder
        charger
        (possibly) storm trooper
    Gameplay
        run and gun, shooting enemy weakspots
    Buildings in downtown
        El Bueno Taco
        Its Better Jerked Jamaican Cuisine
        Dry Cleaning
        Police department
    Environmental hazards
        burning cars and puddles of hellfire limit path
    Pickups
        health pickups
        shield (grit) enhancers
        weapon upgrades

    
SEQUENCE
    Get to downtown from the trailer, see Demon Fascists (DF) breaking into and harrassing a store
        store - El Bueno Taco
    Get into a fight outside and this leads into the store
        Secret inside the store - grit enhancer
    When all of the enemies are beat, a car drives into the fence, letting more enemies into the fight
        also opens up a path to the rest of the level
        car driving into the fence gives the player a path to vault/jump over the fence further into town
    After fight, continue down strip into town
    Next fight occurs near a train that has derailed into a store in the strip
        store - Kids Clothes and Scream Pillows Inc
            irony: train is stopped by a bunch of pillows
            secret in store behind train engine: health enhancement
    train stops progress down strip, so you have to go over a pedestrian bridge to get past the train
    on other side of tracks, have your first fight with demon dogs
        joke: "Release the hounds! I've always wanted to say that"
        takes place in a small parking lot and playground, relatively open
        at other end of open area of fight, there is another ped bridge going back to the strip
    On the second ped bridge, come across your first exploder slowly approaching from the other side of the bridge
    At bottom of ped bridge have another fight in front of the police station
        start feeling the pressure of only having the pistol
        secret: turning towards the train and exploring reveals a pistol dmg upgrade
    Hellfire and burning cars blocks the path down the strip, so you have to go into the police station
    In the police station, its obvious that interior sabotage broke the wall between the police station and their neighbor, Jamaican
        In the police station, find shotgun that was taken by the police
            character moment: Bert hopes that the cop that got him back home and took his shotgun is ok
    In Jamaican, fight some demon dogs and exploders in close quarters, allow shotgun to shine
    Jamaican wall is broken into Confederate Loser Memorial Museum next to it
    Go out back of Museum to downtown parking lot
        secret: in the parking lot, turn left (back towards beginning behind strip) to find a secret
    Go further down strip to DF Forward Operating Base (FOB)
        Introduce chargers and have large scale battle
            use explosive barrels to help with large number of enemies
    After fight, another car runs into the building providing a way to climb to the downtown store roofs
    Fight across roofs to a sign you can knock over onto the train
    Fight over the train, on ceiling and down into open-top train cars, to the caboose
    Lose the DF in the woods on the way to the retirement home
